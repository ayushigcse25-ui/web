Primary Academic Structure

Comprehensive primary education curriculum designed for foundational literacy, numeracy, and holistic child development.

Our Grade Levels

Primary Classes (इयत्ता १ ली ते ५ वी)

Age-appropriate progressive learning outcomes aligned with Maharashtra State Primary Education norms.

इयत्ता १ ली

Class 1

Age: 6 - 7 Years

Foundational entry point: letter recognition, phonics, number sense (1-20), rhymes, drawing, and school adaptation.

इयत्ता २ री

Class 2

Age: 7 - 8 Years

Sentence formation, reading short Marathi & English words, two-digit addition & subtraction, and observation of nature.

इयत्ता ३ री

Class 3

Age: 8 - 9 Years

Fluent reading with comprehension, multiplication/division concepts, basic environmental studies (EVS), and moral stories.

इयत्ता ४ थी

Class 4

Age: 9 - 10 Years

Advanced reading, paragraph writing, geometry basics, local geography, history of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, and science awareness.

इयत्ता ५ वी

Class 5

Age: 10 - 11 Years

Comprehensive primary synthesis: critical thinking, mathematics problem solving, environmental science, and scholarship exam guidance.

Curriculum Overview

Key Learning Areas & Subjects

Structured learning areas designed to cultivate well-rounded academic and personal growth.

📖

Marathi (मातृभाषा मराठी)

Foundational mother tongue development covering reading, clear handwriting, vocabulary, listening comprehension, and creative oral expression.

🔤

English (इंग्रजी)

Basic English alphabet, simple conversational phrases, daily vocabulary, action words, and picture-assisted reading.

🔢

Mathematics (गणित)

Numeracy, basic arithmetic operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division), word problems, shapes, and measurement.

🌱

Environmental Studies (परिसर अभ्यास)

Understanding plants, animals, water conservation, community helpers, sanitation, seasons, and local rural geography.

💡

General Knowledge (सामान्य ज्ञान)

Awareness of our country, Maharashtra state symbols, national leaders, science facts, and daily current events.

🎨

Art & Creativity (कला व हस्तकला)

Free-hand drawing, colouring, origami, clay modeling, and craftwork using locally available eco-friendly materials.

🏃

Physical Education & Yoga (शारीरिक शिक्षण)

Daily morning exercises, basic yoga asanas, team games (kho-kho, kabaddi), hygiene habits, and sportsmanship.

🤝

Moral Values & Life Skills (मूल्यशिक्षण)

Instilling truthfulness, empathy, civic responsibility, respect for elders, community service, and gender equality.

ℹ️ Curriculum Structure Note: All learning areas are structured as per Maharashtra State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT) and Maharashtra State Board guidelines. Textbooks provided free by the Maharashtra State Bureau of Textbook Production (Balbharati).
How We Teach

Our Teaching Approach

Creating a joyful, non-threatening, and engaging classroom environment where every student thrives.

1. Activity-Based Learning (ABL)

Children learn best by doing. We use educational toys, counting beads, flashcards, story cubes, and role-play to turn learning into a playful experience.

2. Conceptual Understanding

Instead of rote memorization, emphasis is laid on understanding core concepts through real-world examples from village life and nature.

3. Active Student Participation

Every child is encouraged to speak freely, ask questions, recite poems, and share their observations during daily classroom discussions.

4. Basic Digital Learning

Incorporating audio-visual educational aids and digital learning materials where available to enrich classroom engagement.

5. Moral & Social Values

Values of honesty, kindness, cooperation, cleanliness, and mutual respect are woven into daily classroom routines and morning assemblies.

6. Continuous Evaluation (CCE)

Following the Continuous and Comprehensive Evaluation framework to gauge child progress through diagnostic observation rather than stress-inducing exams.
