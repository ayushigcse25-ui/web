About Our School

Serving the rural community of Ghorad with quality foundational learning, values, and character development.

Our Story & Background

Nurturing Young Learners in Rural Maharashtra

Zila Parishad Primary School, Ghorad is a government primary school functioning under the Zilla Parishad Nagpur, Department of School Education, Government of Maharashtra.

Located in the village of Ghorad within the Kalmeshwar Taluka of Nagpur district, our school is dedicated to delivering foundational education to young children from Class 1 through Class 5. We serve children and families across the local agricultural and rural community, ensuring that every child, regardless of socio-economic background, has access to joyful, safe, and holistic primary schooling.

School Background & Foundation

Established under the rural local self-government framework of Zilla Parishad Nagpur, the school follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) along with foundational learning directives from the State Council of Educational Research and Training (SCERT Maharashtra).

Pedagogy & Society

Educational Approach & Role in the Local Community

Empowering children with holistic learning while serving as an active social hub for the village.

Child-Centric Pedagogy

We believe in joyful, interactive, and activity-based learning. Abstract concepts in math, language, and science are explained using everyday objects, flashcards, charts, and peer learning activities.

School Management Committee (SMC)

Parents, local Gram Panchayat representatives, and teachers regularly meet to review school infrastructure, mid-day meal cleanliness, student attendance, and community welfare initiatives.

Inclusion & Equality

Providing a completely free, non-discriminatory, and safe educational environment where boys and girls from all backgrounds learn and grow together with equal dignity and equal opportunities.
