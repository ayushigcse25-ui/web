About Our School

Dedicated to Foundational Education & Community Upliftment

Zila Parishad Primary School, Ghorad plays a pivotal role in providing accessible, high-quality foundational primary education to young children in the village of Ghorad and surrounding rural communities in Kalmeshwar Taluka, Nagpur District.

Our school strives to create a warm, inclusive, and supportive learning environment where foundational literacy and numeracy (FLN) are nurtured alongside strong moral character, curiosity, discipline, and mutual respect.

Our Foundation

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding every step in our journey to impart meaningful and value-centric primary education.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of quality primary education where every rural child receives equitable learning opportunities, develops critical thinking, cultivates sound moral values, and builds self-confidence for lifelong success and responsible citizenship.

  • Fostering foundational literacy and numeracy for all students
  • Instilling respect for Indian heritage, culture, and environmental harmony
  • Nurturing curiosity, ethical leadership, and creative expression

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, child-friendly, and participatory education that empowers young learners with fundamental academic skills, healthy living habits, social harmony, and a strong foundation for future schooling.

  • Ensuring 100% enrollment and joyful retention in primary classes
  • Implementing interactive, activity-based pedagogy and experiential learning
  • Encouraging active community and parental partnership through the School Management Committee (SMC)
